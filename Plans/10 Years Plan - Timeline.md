# Jobs

1. Quantum Computing 
**Why this?:** It breaks the 'bound' that traditional computing has a gives a *exponentially* $(2^N)$ higher computational force

**Skills needed:**

	- Mathematics 
		- Linear algebra, probability theory and complex numbers 
	- Quantum Mechanics
			- Strong intuitive grasp on things: superposition, entanglement, interference, and quantum gates
			- In summary, the framework of quantum mechanics.
		- Classical Computer Science
			- Strong proficiency in:
				- Algorithms
				- Data structures
				- Programming languages

**What to do now?**
*Mathwise:*
1. Abstract algebra (**Key**)
2. Discrete Math & Graph Theory 
3. Probability and Random Processes 

*Physics*
1. Intro to Quantum Mechanics 
2. Statistical physics
3. Quantum Information
4. 固体物理或原子分子光物理（AMO)

*Computer science*
1. Python, C++, Rust
2. Algorithem and Data Structure(How computers work)
3. Quantum Algorithms-
	1. **Grover 算法**
	2. **Shor 算法**
	3. **VQE（变分量子特征求解器）和 QAOA**（在近期有噪声的中等规模量子设备 NISQ 上最有可能商业化的优化算法）。
