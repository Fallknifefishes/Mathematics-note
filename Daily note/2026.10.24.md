## **💡 Insight**
Consider a function $f: A\to B$,
- Surjectivity; $\forall b\in B, \exists a \in A$ such that $f(a)=b$ is a constrain of the codomain
- Right inverse is $Id_{b}$, so surjectivity of $f$ ensures the existance of right invers
- Essentially the proof of the is pick elements from preimage set of $b \in B$, and essentially there is a theorem saying that
> Every surjective function $f$ has a right inverse $\iff$ Axoim of Choice

- Right and left inverses are *not* unique, but if they both exists then they are the same function, and therefore inverse function, and thus unique **by the uniqueness of inverse**

- sujectivity = Right inverse, Injectivity = Left inverse

- Think of the proof as *drawing diagrams and picking bijective subsets under $f$ .*
