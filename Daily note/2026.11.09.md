## **💡 Insight**
- Essentially the 'random variable' is assigning a value to each data point in the outcome space. So that we could study probability of the value occuring, instead of the actual data point

- $\mathbb{P}(X = x) = \mathbb{P}(\{\omega|X(\omega) =x\}$ is the preimage of $x$ by the mapping $X$

- $\mathbb{P}(X \leq a) = F_{X}(a)$ is the cumulative distribution function

- Looking at descrete random varible; this limits the 'freedom', let the **support** of the distribution $supp(X) = \{x\in \mathbb{R}| P(X=x) >0\}$, be the actual 'meaningful' data points that has positive probability. And is what we consider about. **Intuitionally**, 'support' is the **point of inconsecutivity** on the cumulative distribution diagram, it kind of 'supports' the diagram to 'stand'.
