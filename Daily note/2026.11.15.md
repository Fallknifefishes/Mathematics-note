- Note: Elements in the ideal **takes** elements from $R$, by multiplication, inside the ideal. It **obsorbs through multiplication**. This is suprisingly similar to how **idealism** works in the society.
- **Unit group**: A very special group structure inside a ring; Elements of those who is invertable under multiplication in a ring
