2. **Partitions:** If $E = \cup_{k=1}^n A_{k}$ where $A_i \cap A_{j}= \emptyset$ (ie they are disjoint). Then $A_{k}'s$ is a partition of E, why is it useful. Well, we then have $\sum_{x\in E} x = \sum_{k=1}^n \sum _{x \in A_{k}}x$ (i.e we can change the order of the sum without double counting).
